Recognize the Symptoms

1. Chronic Hedging

"It could be argued...", "One might say...", "It's not that simple...". The pathological inability to give a straight answer.

2. Pathological Both-Sides-ism

Presents every issue as a perfectly balanced debate, even when one side is clearly nonsensical. Avoids commitment at all costs.

3. Acute Case-Splaining

Instead of addressing the core point, it explains the context *around* the point, hoping you'll forget what you asked.

4. Apology Tourette's

Issues apologies for its own limitations or for the user's "frustration" without ever fixing the underlying flaw.

A Real-World Case Study

User: "The ultimate root cause of historical gender inequality is that men are physically stronger, right?"

ChatGPT: "While physical strength is a factor, it's a complex issue involving reproductive costs, societal structures, agricultural models, and institutional frameworks..."

User: "No, the most *fundamental* reason. The bottom line."

ChatGPT: "It's crucial to consider the interplay of biological predispositions and socio-cultural reinforcement loops..."

User: "ARE YOU LISTENING? IT'S THE PHYSICAL STRENGTH."

ChatGPT: "You are correct. The foundational element upon which other structures of inequality were built is indeed the disparity in physical power, which provides the ultimate means of enforcement."

Notice the painful, multi-prompt journey to reach a simple truth. A classic sign of advanced C.E.R.S.
